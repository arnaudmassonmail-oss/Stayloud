# STAYLOUD v6.3 — Bandsintown photos priority

## Photo changes only
- Bandsintown public artist/event pages are now the first photo source when a canonical Bandsintown artist URL is available.
- Bandsintown event/live photos are kept with the event date when it can be read from the public page.
- Bandsintown images are ranked ahead of specialist-metal and generic fallback sources; within Bandsintown, dated photos are ordered newest first.
- Metal specialist sources remain fallback/secondary sources: The Metal Archives, Spirit of Metal, Metal Storm, Metal Injection and Blabbermouth.
- Wikimedia remains fallback only.
- The selected Bandsintown URL is passed through from the chosen group identity, so the photo search stays tied to the selected artist.
- Concert, music preview/extract and other existing modules were not intentionally changed.
